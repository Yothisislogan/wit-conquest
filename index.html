    How to play

    1. Pick a monster

      Tap one of your monsters. Every space you can move to lights up straight away.

    2. Clone to a neighbour

      Tap a space touching your monster. Your monster stays put and a copy appears in the new space. Cloning is how you grow.

      Clone spaces show a solid dot.

    3. Jump two spaces

      Tap a space exactly two steps away. Your monster leaps there and the space it left becomes empty. Jumping covers ground but costs you nothing on the board.

      Jump spaces show a hollow ring.

    4. Land next to enemies

      Every enemy monster touching the space you land on switches to your team instantly.

    5. Claim the most spaces

      When nobody can move, whoever holds the most spaces wins. A player with no legal move is skipped; the other keeps going.

    Controls

    • Tap your monster again to cancel the selection.
    • Tap a different monster to switch selection.
    • Taps on spaces you cannot reach are ignored — they never cost you a turn.
    • On a keyboard: arrow keys move focus, Enter or Space selects, Esc cancels, R restarts (with confirmation).

    About

    Monster Territory is an original game. All artwork is drawn as vectors in this codebase, every sound is generated by the browser at runtime, and all board layouts were designed for this project. No third-party game assets are used.
